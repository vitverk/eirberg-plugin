# Support

For installation-package bugs, [open an issue](https://github.com/vitverk/eirberg-plugin/issues/new).
Include the package version, the host you used, the failed step and a redacted error message.

For staff access, permissions or store operations, contact your existing Eirberg/Vitverk
administrator through the established internal support channel. Public issues cannot grant
store access. The independent `https://mcp.eirberg.app/mcp` service is deployed against
Medusa staging. Installing the package does not establish a staff OAuth connection or
verify that account's permissions. If tools are unavailable, include the host's connection
status and exact redacted error; do not infer an account refusal from missing tools alone.

Do not post customer records, order/payment details, private media, cookies, passwords,
tokens or unredacted screenshots in public issues.
